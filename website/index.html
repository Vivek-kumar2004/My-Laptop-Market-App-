ABOUT US

Building India's Trusted B2B Network for the IT Industry

My Laptop Market is a B2B networking and lead-generation platform for the wider IT industry. We connect Refurbished IT Dealers India, Technology Suppliers, IT Product Distributors, wholesalers, importers, exporters, ITAD companies, enterprise hardware dealers, and service providers through one credible business ecosystem.

Our mission is to make vendor discovery faster, business visibility stronger, and industry collaboration more reliable. From regional IT dealers to national distributors and Corporate IT Equipment sourcing teams, the platform helps companies find relevant partners, create qualified conversations, and grow with confidence.
