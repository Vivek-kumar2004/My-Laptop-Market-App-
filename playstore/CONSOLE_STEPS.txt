My Laptop Market — Play Console steps (jo Google account se aapko karna hai)
===========================================================================

Yeh machine par jo kaam ho sakta tha, wo ho chuka hai:
- targetSdk 36 (Play ki 31 Aug 2026 requirement)
- signed Android App Bundle (.aab)
- privacy policy HTML
- listing text + 512 icon + feature graphic

Aapke Google account / ₹25 fee ke bina Play Console mein app live NAHI ho sakti.
Neeche wale steps aap khud complete karein.

1) Developer account
   - https://play.google.com/console
   - Google account se login (faiz@mylaptopmarket.com ya jo business account hai)
   - One-time registration fee ~USD 25
   - Identity verification (PAN/Aadhaar/GST jaisa Google maange)
   - Payments profile: India, business details, address

2) Privacy policy ko website par daalein (REQUIRED)
   File: playstore/privacy-policy.html
   Isko apni hosting par upload karein taaki ye URL khule:
     https://mylaptopmarket.com/privacy-policy.html
   Browser mein open karke confirm karein page load ho.

3) Naya app banao
   Play Console → Create app
   - App name: My Laptop Market
   - Default language: English (United States) or English (India)
   - App or game: App
   - Free
   - Declarations accept karein

4) Store listing
   - playstore/listing.txt se copy-paste
   - App icon: playstore/assets/icon-512.png
   - Feature graphic: playstore/assets/feature-graphic.png
   - Phone screenshots: KAM SE KAM 2 (recommended 4–8)
     Real device ya emulator se lo: Home, product detail, search, vendor add product
     Size: JPEG/PNG, 16:9 or 9:16, 320px se 3840px
   - Privacy policy URL paste karein

5) App bundle upload
   File:
     app/build/outputs/bundle/release/app-release.aab
   Play Console → Test and release → Production (ya pehle Internal testing)
   Google Play App Signing: "Let Google manage" select karein (recommended)
   Upload key yeh wala keystore hai: keystore/mylaptopmarket-upload.jks
   KEYSTORE KO KABHI MAT KHO DENA — bina iske update nahi ja sakta.

6) App content forms
   - Privacy policy
   - Ads: No
   - Target audience: 18+
   - News: No
   - COVID: No
   - Data safety: listing.txt ke hisaab se
   - Government / political: No
   - Financial features: No (vendor subscription website par hai, in-app Play Billing nahi)
   - Health: No
   - App access: test vendor username/password do, warna reviewer login nahi kar payega
   - Content rating: IARC questionnaire complete karo
   - Store settings: category Shopping, email, phone, address

7) Countries
   India select karo (aur jo markets chahiye)

8) Submit
   Internal testing pehle bhejo, install karke check karo, phir Production → Send for review
   Review 1–7 din lag sakta hai.

KEYSTORE BACKUP
  Files (git mein nahi jaate):
    keystore/mylaptopmarket-upload.jks
    keystore.properties
    playstore/KEYSTORE_BACKUP.txt
  Inko Google Drive / password manager / USB par copy karke rakho.
