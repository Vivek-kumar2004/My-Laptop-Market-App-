Full description:
My Laptop Market is India’s B2B network for IT vendors, dealers, distributors and buyers.

Browse live listings of laptops, desktops, monitors, servers, storage, graphics cards and accessories from verified businesses across India. Search by product, connect with shops, and send requirements when you need stock.

Vendors can register, complete a short verification subscription, add products with photos and videos, chat with buyers, and manage listings from the phone.

Features
• Browse and search verified vendor listings
• Categories: Laptops, Desktop, Screens, Server, Storage, Accessories, Graphics Cards
• Vendor registration, profile and shop logo
• Add, edit and remove product listings
• In-app messaging and buyer requirements
• Optional voice assistant for product questions
• Separate admin login for marketplace staff

This app is for business users in the IT trade, not a consumer retail store. Physical hardware is transacted between businesses. Vendor verification payments are completed on our website.
